# Rebuttal format & style guide (author-mandated 2026-07-27; template = Hymba ICLR'25 #1217)

## Structure (what gets posted, in order)
1. **"Thank All Reviewers and General Response"** — top-level Official Comment. For us this
   doubles as the reply to the AC meta-review (which pre-dates the rebuttal). Contents:
   sincere thanks; one-para summary of what the rebuttal adds (all on the submitted
   configuration); a **"Strengths commented by the reviewers"** block quoting each
   reviewer's own positive words, grouped by theme (Hymba pattern).
2. **One independent reply per reviewer** ("Response to Reviewer XXXX"), each self-contained:
   - Opening: "We sincerely thank the reviewer for their time and constructive comments!
     We appreciate the reviewer's recognition of ..." + 2–4 QUOTED fragments from THEIR
     review (their exact words in quotation marks).
   - Then Q-by-Q: a bold header per item ("**Q1: <short topic>**"), the reviewer's question
     QUOTED (indented/italic), then the answer — only THEIR questions, answered with OUR
     data. Tables inline (no links; NO external URLs).
   - Split into "(Part 2)"/"(Part 3)" comments when long (OpenReview length limits).
   - Close with what we commit to include in the revision.
3. **Cross-reviewer citations with credit ("cite and bravo")**: whenever an answer uses
   material another reviewer prompted, name and credit them, e.g. "the calibrated
   static-TPOT alternative proposed by Reviewer gTfM (exactly the right probe)"; "the
   power-of-two-choices dispatcher suggested by Reviewer faAU". Hymba's own example:
   "As pointed out by other reviewers as well, e.g., 'an innovative approach' by
   Reviewer HFsT..." — use this to let strengths echo across replies.

## Style rules
- Warm, direct, zero defensiveness; agree where the reviewer is right ("We agree with your
  reading, and will state the scoping plainly in the revision").
- Every number from banked, audited cells on the SUBMITTED configuration; honest negatives
  reported plainly (our λ30-stationary row) — they buy credibility for the wins.
- Commit-to-revision sentences for every writing item ("we have added / we will include ...
  in the revised manuscript").
- Forthcoming results promised only per plan (model family, topology) — Hymba pattern:
  "we will share our results with the community once they are available."
- Post the FIRST responses from this packet, then hold the cluster live and answer
  follow-ups quickly (Hymba's score raises came from the follow-up exchanges: 6→8, 5→6,
  6→8 — responsiveness is the mechanism).

## Our rails on top of the template (unchanged, binding)
NO links · no codename · no version/build language (v1 rebuttal only; hide v2 totally) ·
one RB preset per table · v1exact basis for every paper-comparable number · no k=50 · no
BEST-Route-frontier/t05 re-assertions · Pareto-shaped claims only vs single-model refs ·
quality margins ≤0.01 only with paired CI (now computed) · gamma-only burst support ·
sq40/6 never reviewer-facing · dual-basis internal check silent.
