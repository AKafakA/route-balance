## What each post claims, and the evidence behind it

**POST 1 — 287j (writing, batching, KV/TTFT).** Two-layer design (RB assigns instances; vLLM
keeps continuous batching), the adaptive batch-size step schedule (ratio <0.3 → 4/50 ms;
<0.7 → 16/100 ms; else 32/200 ms), disabling adaptivity costs 5.1–6.9% e2e, and a list of
revision commitments (Figure 1 redraw, appendix walkthrough, LPT pseudocode, dead-reckoning
defined at first use).

**POST 2 — gTfM (estimator).** The calibrated static comparator across 7 rates: learned head
8.1–9.8% cheaper per 1k requests, quality indistinguishable (all seven paired CIs span zero,
widest [−0.0063, +0.0014]), throughput within 0.4%, mean e2e within 2.7%. Third arm removing
the latency term entirely: load-blind routing, 69.1% smallest tier, quality 0.4899 against
0.504–0.510. Cells: `v1exact_bridge` (learned), `v1exact_recal` (static), `v1exact_timematch`
(the paired latency rows at λ 12/24/28).

**POST 3 — faAU (dispatchers, generality, cold start).** W3 dispatcher table (`po2`,
`po2_routers`); W1-A re-provisioned topology (`topology_flip_v1x`); W1-B Gemma-3 family
(`gemma_cells`); W2-A label/prompt-blind ladder against dispatchers (`v1exact_ladder_1sess`
plus `po2`); W2-B onboarding cost (404,929 profiling requests over 11.1 h, all five pairings
concurrent; Gemma generation ~6 h, judging ~2 h, index 52.3 s).
